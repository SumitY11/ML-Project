Soil Health & Nitrogen Efficiency

Optimizing Nitrogen-Phosphorus-Potassium ratios prevents soil degradation and increases fertilizer recovery by up to 25%.

  • Soil pH balancing with lime or gypsum
  • Targeted foliar sprays during tillering
  • Leguminous crop rotation for natural nitrogen fixation
🌧️

Climate & Flood-Drought Management

Mitigate volatile monsoon seasons through adaptive field practices and water retention techniques.

  • Drip irrigation to buffer dry spells
  • Field bunding and runoff harvesting ponds
  • Short-duration flood-tolerant seed varieties
🧪

Integrated Pest & Disease Control

Combine biological control with minimal chemical interventions to protect beneficial soil micro-organisms.

  • Neem-based organic sprays for early pest stages
  • Pheromone traps for moth and worm monitoring
  • Clean weeding during initial crop germination
🌾

Post-Harvest Storage & Quality

Prevent moisture degradation and fungal growth during storage to retain market value.

  • Moisture content reduction to below 12% before bagging
  • Hermetic storage bags for pest prevention
  • Cool, ventilated warehouse positioning
